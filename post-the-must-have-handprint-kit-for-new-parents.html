The Must-Have Handprint Kit for New Parents

Ashley Eccleston 14 July 2025 · 6 min read

There's something magical about watching your newborn's tiny hands wave in the air or grasp your thumb with surprising strength.

However, in the quiet of the night, you realise just how quickly these moments will slip away.

As parents ourselves, we get it. But with our foil baby handprint kit, you can hold onto these precious memories forever.

Here's everything you need to know about creating our beautiful milestone keepsakes.

What Is a Foil Baby Handprint Kit?

Our baby handprint kits come with everything required to capture a stunningly clear and detailed foil print of your baby's hand or footprints.

They are thoughtfully designed to be quick, mess-free, and stress-free, ideal for parents grappling with sleepless nights and a wriggling infant!

And you don't even have to create the prints yourself. We do all the hard work for you.

All you have to do is simply take the print, snap a photo, and send it to us. Then, using our special printing process, we transform your baby's handprints into a bespoke foil print and post it to you.

The hardest part, really, is deciding where to display your new keepsake.

Why Our Baby Handprint Kits Are One of a Kind

So, why choose us over the many other companies that provide foil prints?

Here are the three key reasons why our kits are a cut above the rest.

1. Foil Fusion Technology™

The first reason has everything to do with how our foil prints are made.

We found that traditional printing methods were flawed and outdated. The process of glueing foil to cardstock often leaves a speckled finish that lacks the premium quality you'd expect from a cherished keepsake. Even more concerning, these prints tend to fade over time and eventually begin peeling away from the card.

As you might agree, this is hardly the lasting memento most parents desire.

Our customers deserve milestone keepsakes that look flawless for life, so we worked tirelessly to develop a new method we now call Foil Fusion Technology™.

This advanced method fuses a specialised foil directly onto carefully selected cardstock using a seamless, one-step process. The result? A pristine, high-gloss finish with no speckles, no glue, and no imperfections. The foil bonds permanently to the card, ensuring your baby's handprint never peels or fades, even after decades of being displayed.

To date, The Bespoke Foil Company is the only place where you can get this kind of result.

2. Truly eco-friendly

Like many parents, we're deeply mindful of the world we're leaving behind for our children and future generations.

Therefore, we didn't want to sell a product that contributed to landfills or pollution in any way.

That's why we've eliminated all plastic from our packaging and ensured that every element of our baby handprint kits is recyclable. The sturdy kit box can be reused as storage for prints and other photos, and even the cello wallet our prints are delivered in is compostable.

Unlike plastic toys and baby clothes, which are quickly outgrown and cast aside, our prints offer an eco-conscious and sustainable option that lasts a lifetime.

This makes our baby handprint kits the perfect gift for anyone who cares about the environment and wants to choose something meaningful yet kind to our planet.

3. A family business

The Bespoke Foil Company is made for parents, by parents.

When you choose us, you support an independent, family business. We've built our company from the ground up, and we pour our heart and soul into everything we do.

To us, you're not just a number on a system. We genuinely care about each customer's experience. That's why every improvement and innovation comes directly from listening to parents like you.

When you contact us, you interact with real humans, not automated bots, who are dedicated to ensuring you're completely happy with your print.

From our family to yours, we're honoured to help you preserve these precious parenting memories in a way that truly does them justice.

What's in the Baby Handprint Kit?

Not a lot (but that's a good thing)!

We don't want to overwhelm you with overly complicated processes, so we've designed our inkless handprint kits to be as straightforward as possible. And, we think we've achieved exactly that.

In the kit you'll find:

  • Two inkless hand wipes
  • Four sheets of reactive paper
  • A comprehensive instruction sheet (including how to purchase additional print copies and other upgrades or extras)
  • Your order number card (you'll need this when uploading the photos)

And that's it! All you need is your baby and a few minutes.

Everything comes in a reusable, branded box that fits perfectly through the letterbox, so no trips to the sorting office are required.

How to Use Our Baby Handprint Kits

Creating your keepsake takes just five minutes.

For best results, choose a time when your baby is calm and relaxed, like after a feeding or even while your little one is asleep.

Open the box and lay out all the contents. You'll need a solid surface (you can use the box the kit came in). Then, here's how it works:

  1. Take one of the mess-free, inkless wipes and apply it to your baby's hand. Make sure you cover it thoroughly. Use the second wipe if you need to (though it's usually not necessary).
  2. Apply the hand gently yet firmly to the reactive paper and watch as the handprint magically appears. We recommend taking a couple of prints without reapplying the wipe to allow us to select the best one to use for printing.
  3. Then, take a photo of the prints and upload them to our website. At this point, you can choose your personalisation options (text, foil and frame colour, etc.).

Now, relax and let us handle the rest.

Once we receive the prints, we'll tidy them up and get rid of any smudges. Then, we'll transform them into your personalised foil artwork.

Before we dispatch the print (free of charge), we'll send you proofs for approval.

Foil Handprint Kits vs. Other Methods

Naturally, foil printing isn't the only way to create a newborn keepsake, but we believe our baby handprint kit offers one of the simplest methods with the most durable results.

Some popular alternatives include:

  • DIY ink or paint printing: Many new parents opt for a DIY approach using ink or paint. While it's inexpensive and easy to do at home, it can be messy and difficult to get a clear print. Ink and paint can smudge easily, and some products may irritate a baby's sensitive skin or cause allergic reactions if not carefully chosen.
  • Clay imprints: Clay offers a nice 3D effect. However, it often requires firm pressure to capture a detailed impression, which isn't ideal for delicate baby hands, and the results can lack definition. And not to mention, clay is fragile; one accidental drop could ruin your keepsake.
  • Plaster casting: Plaster kits can create impressively lifelike 3D models. But keeping a baby still long enough for casting is tricky, and the final result can feel a little unsettling due to its "disembodied" look. Like clay, plaster is fragile and must be stored carefully to avoid damage.
  • Professional printing: Many companies offer foil handprint services, but not all are equal. Most use the aforementioned outdated methods that can lead to speckled or uneven results. Over time, these prints may peel or fade, especially if not sealed or finished correctly.

In contrast, our hand and footprint kits are gentle, durable, and designed with both baby and parent in mind, delivering a keepsake that stands the test of time.

FAQs

What is a handprint kit used for?

An inkless handprint kit captures flawless prints of your baby's hand in a stress-free, mess-free way.

Using our special wipe, you apply your baby's hand or foot to the reactive paper. After uploading a photograph of the print, we transform it into a beautiful foil keepsake.

Why is a handprint kit ideal for newborn parents?

An inkless handprint kit is ideal for newborn parents because it's an incredibly fast and simple way to create a lasting reminder of how tiny their baby's hands are.

The process only takes a few minutes and causes no stress to the baby. The kit is dispatched to your home, making it a perfect option for busy parents who don't want the hassle or mess of traditional handprint methods.

Is a handprint kit safe to use on a newborn?

Yes! Our baby handprint kits are completely safe to use on even the tiniest of newborn babies. You can even do it while still in the hospital.

The inkless wipe is non-toxic, contains no nasty chemicals or parabens, and won't irritate or damage a newborn's delicate skin.

How many foil prints can I get?

The baby handprint kit includes one A4 print in your choice of coloured foil. However, you can order as many additional prints as you like.

The instruction sheet that comes with the kit contains details on how to purchase extras.

Are your foil prints expensive?

No! Our prices are competitive, and each inkless handprint kit costs less than £50. Adding a frame at checkout increases the cost slightly, but you are welcome to omit this if you prefer to frame the print yourself.
