Step-by-Step Guide to a Beautiful Baby Keepsake

Ashley Eccleston 14 July 2025 · 6 min read

Our foil print kits let you preserve your newborn's tiny hands and feet in a truly special way, creating a wonderful keepsake you can enjoy for a lifetime.

Here's our super-easy, DIY guide on how to use our kits and create lasting memories.

Why Foil Prints Make Treasured Keepsakes

As you marvel at your new baby's tiny features, you might wonder how to capture those special moments for eternity. Of course, you take a million pictures, but they fail to capture the true size and intricate details of your child's tiny hands and feet.

That's where our memory-making foil prints are different.

They preserve the exact size and shape of your baby's hands and feet in shimmering, detailed foil. A fleeting moment is transformed into a lasting piece of art you can cherish forever, keeping your memories safe.

Our foil hand and footprint kits are simple to use and are perfectly safe for newborns. That means you can take the prints as soon as your little one arrives, capturing the hands and feet at their tiniest.

How to Create Baby Keepsakes in 6 Steps

To get started, all you need is your baby, the kit, and a firm surface. If needed, the sturdy box the kit arrives in doubles as a reliable surface for taking prints.

1. Wait until your baby is calm

Find a calm moment for you and your baby. After a feeding is ideal, or you can even take the prints while your little one is snoozing.

If you're taking footprints, remove anything covering the feet. If it's cold, keep a blanket wrapped around your baby to keep them cosy.

2. Remove the items from the box

Grab the box and remove the contents. Inside the kit, you'll find:

  • 2 inkless wipes
  • 4 sheets of reactive paper
  • A card with your order number on it
  • A detailed instruction booklet plus information on how to order add-ons and extra prints

3. Apply the inkless wipe

The kit enables you to take prints of one of the following:

  • Two hands
  • Two feet
  • One hand and one foot

Decide which option you prefer before removing one of the inkless wipes from its packaging.

Apply it thoroughly to your baby's hands and/or feet. We also included a second wipe as a backup, just in case. Once you have applied it to your baby, there's no need to reapply.

4. Press onto the reactive paper

Using a firm surface (or the kit box), gently press your little one's feet or hands onto the reactive paper. An imprint will magically appear!

Go ahead and use all four sheets. The more prints we receive, the easier it is to select the best ones for the finished result.

5. Snap and upload the photos

The hard part is over!

When you have a moment to yourself, snap a photo of each sheet of reactive paper.

The important part is to ensure that all four sides of the paper are within the shot so we can view the entire thing. This allows us to adjust the image so it accurately captures the size of those teeny hands and feet.

For this reason, please don't take close-up images of the prints without having the sides of the paper in shot. Otherwise, we can't determine the true size of the prints.

Next, upload the photos to our website. You'll also need to input your order number that arrived in the kit.

At this point, you can also choose your personalisation options and extras.

Here's what you can do:

  • Add any text you like (baby's name, birth info, a special quote, etc.)
  • Pick the font of the text
  • Order extra copies of the print
  • Add a frame upgrade

6. Wait for your keepsake to arrive

Once the photos are uploaded, relax and let us do the rest.

We'll select the best prints and tidy them up by removing any smudges and other imperfections. Then, you'll receive the final result for approval via WhatsApp or email.

When you give us the thumbs up, we'll go ahead and transform them into stunning foil prints.

Finally, we send the print to your door, free of charge.

All that's left to do now is decide where to hang your beautiful baby keepsake.

Should You DIY a Baby Keepsake?

DIY keepsakes are a great way to save a little money, but they do have some drawbacks.

For instance, taking ink prints of your baby's hands and feet is messy, and the ink might irritate the baby's skin. Plaster casting is also messy, and the finished result can break if you're not careful around it.

In contrast, foil prints are mess-free and durable. However, you need special equipment to create them yourself. That's why it's best to choose a high-quality, professional company to do it for you.

If you'd still like to make something yourself, a DIY baby book is a great and easy option. Or, start a memory box filled with keepsake items.

FAQs

How do I use a hand & footprint kit?

You use a hand and footprint kit by applying the inkless wipe to your baby's hands or feet, and then pressing them onto the special reactive paper. Once you have enough imprints, you take photos of the reactive paper and upload them to our website.

We'll transform the prints into gorgeous foil prints and send them directly to your house.

Is it easy for non-crafty people?

Yes! You don't have to be crafty or creative to use our foil print kits. All you have to do is take your little one's hand or footprints and leave the rest up to us.

Our kits are the perfect way for parents to get a special baby keepsake without having to be creative.

What if my baby moves during the process?

If your baby moves during the process, no worries. We included the extra inkless wipe and four sheets of reactive paper for that very reason.

Use as many of the kit items as you need to. Cover the reactive paper with multiple prints. Even if your baby moves, you're still highly likely to get a few great imprints.

And if there are smudges, we can work our magic and remove them, leaving you with clear and perfect images.

Do foil hand and footprint kits make good baby shower gifts?

Yes! Foil hand and footprint kits make excellent baby shower gifts because they're unique and deeply personal.

During pregnancy, expectant parents often receive a lot of practical items, but a keepsake like this stands out as something special. It's a thoughtful gesture that adds a personal touch beyond the typical pregnancy essentials, providing a way to capture special memories once the newborn baby arrives.

Final Thoughts

Those first years will go by in a flash, and it's easy to forget how small those hands and feet once were.

Our foil print kits provide a beautiful, lasting way to preserve these fleeting moments, capturing a piece of your newborn's magic you'll cherish always.
