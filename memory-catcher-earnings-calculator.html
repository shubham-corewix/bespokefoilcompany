Private call-out visits

0/ week
05 / week

A private visit to capture one baby's print, one-to-one — easily slotted in after a morning session. A £10 call-out charge is our suggestion and entirely at your discretion; if you'd rather not add one, that's absolutely fine — but for this calculator we'll assume £10.

Another way to do it — home visits Families can also come to you, in the comfort of your own home. It's where we found a lot of our early success with the Foil Hand & Footprint service — no travel time, no call-out charge, and you can run babies back-to-back. Ashley used to average around five a day in 30-minute slots — customers reached out to her directly on Facebook to book their slot, and she did this at the weekend while still in her old salaried job, before going full-time and setting up The Bespoke Foil Company.
