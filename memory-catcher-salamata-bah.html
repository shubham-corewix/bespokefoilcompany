Six simple steps

From kit to keepsake in minutes

Our inkless kit takes the stress out of capturing those precious prints - just press, print and preserve, then leave the rest to us. Your bit is done in minutes.

01

Receive your kit

Your kit arrives with everything you need - inkless wipes, reactive paper and simple instructions. No mess, no fuss.

02

Capture those tiny prints

Gently press your little one's hand or foot onto the reactive paper. The inkless wipes are safe, and leave no residue on skin.

03

Snap a quick photo

Photograph your prints with all four corners of the paper visible. This allows us to make them true to size.

04

Upload and personalise

Use our simple upload tool to send us your prints. Choose your foil colour, card style and add any personalisation details.

05

We perfect your prints

Our team tidies any smudges whilst keeping every precious detail. We'll send you a proof to approve before we foil.

06

Treasure forever

Once approved, we foil your keepsake and post it back to you free of charge. Ready to display, ready to cherish.

the finish

That never fades

Our own Foil Fusion™ Technology, developed in our Wigan workshop, permanently bonds real metallic foil to high‑quality archival card. No fading, no peeling, no speckling over time. The keepsake you open today will look just as stunning when your little one's all grown up.

Ensuring your prints last a lifetime

Frequently asked questions

We understand how important these moments are, and we're honoured to be part of how you choose to remember them. Below, you'll find answers to some of the most common questions we receive. If you still need help, we're just a message away and always happy to talk things through with you.

Is it safe for newborns?

Completely. The inkless wipes are non‑toxic and hypoallergenic, safe for newborns from day one, and the reactive paper is free from chemicals, dyes and alcohol. Nothing touches your baby's skin except the gentle wipe, and it leaves no residue at all. You can even take prints while your baby sleeps.

What if my baby moves during the process?

No pressure and no one‑shot panic. Your kit includes two wipes and four sheets of reactive paper, so you have several attempts, and the paper captures every tiny detail even if your little one wriggles. Any smudges are tidied up by our team before foiling, and you approve a free digital proof before we create your keepsake.

How many prints can I make with one kit?

Each kit includes two inkless wipes and four sheets of reactive paper, giving you multiple attempts to capture prints you love. Your finished keepsake can feature one hand and one foot, two feet, or two hands - which also makes it lovely for siblings.

Can I order this as a gift before the baby arrives?

Absolutely - it's one of the most popular baby shower gifts we make. The Premium Kit arrives in a luxury presentation box, completely gift‑ready with a keepsake affirmation card, and the new parents simply take the prints whenever they're ready after baby arrives. The kit doesn't expire.

Can I personalise my print?

Yes - and there's nothing to decide today. When you upload your prints, you'll choose your card colour (black or white), your foil colour (gold, silver or rose gold), your frame colour (five options on Framed and Premium kits) and add your personalisation - name, birth details or a special message.

How long until I receive my finished keepsake?

Kits ordered before noon ship the same working day. Once you've uploaded your prints and approved your free digital proof, your finished foil keepsake is created and posted back to you free of charge within five working days - tracked on Framed and Premium kits. There's a small postage charge on the initial kit; returning your finished keepsake costs nothing.
