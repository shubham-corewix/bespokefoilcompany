## 2. ROUTING AND DISCOVERY (the spine of the site)

- `_redirects` is the SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH for routes AND sitemap metadata. Grammar per line: `/path /file.html 200 # Category | Label | optional-llms-note`. `# exclude` keeps a live route out of discovery files. `# util | Label` files it under Utility Pages.
- `sitemap.xml`, `sitemap.html`, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt` are AUTO-GENERATED by `scripts/generate-sitemaps.js` on every deploy. NEVER hand-edit them.
- `scripts/check-internal-links.js` validates every internal href against `_redirects` on every deploy (report-only; `--strict` fails CI). It understands wildcard routes and dynamic JS-built links for these prefixes: `/post/`, `/franchises/`, `/franchises-bio/`, `/memory-catcher/`.
- New page workflow: build page, add ONE `_redirects` line with its comment. Everything else regenerates itself.

## 8. HARD-WON RULES (do not relearn these)

- Pages merged from standalone deploys get asset paths ABSOLUTISED before routing at nested paths - relative paths 404 at any depth below root.
- Verify file identity after merges (size, token, marker strings), not just links. A failing `cp` in a command chain can silently skip critical writes.
- Test over HTTP, never `file://` - absolute paths fail locally by design.
- The zip must be flat with files at root - nested folders 404 the whole deploy.
- Sitemap/discovery files: never hand-edit; the generator owns them.
- QA runs through the snag bookmarklet at `/snag-tool` (exports JSON with page, selector, viewport, note). Fixes logged in `BFC-WEBSITE-THREAD.md` with a running changelog.
