Being Eco-Friendly

A greener future for tiny hands & feet

At The Bespoke Foil Company, we're making real changes to reduce our environmental impact, because we believe that beautiful things shouldn't come at a cost to the planet.

No more plastic.
No unnecessary waste.

From the thick, premium card we print on to the solid frames we offer, our products are designed to last a lifetime.

Fully Recyclable

All our packaging is now 100% plastic-free. That means no bubble wrap, no shrink wrap, no plastic sleeves. Instead, we've moved to fully recyclable and responsibly sourced materials that still protect your precious prints in transit, without harming the environment.

We've taken care to find packaging that feels as thoughtful and well-crafted as the keepsakes inside. So when your order arrives, it feels like a gift you can feel proud of in every sense.

It's not about being perfect. It's about doing better.

We'll be honest. We're not a big corporation with a full sustainability department. We're just a small family business trying to make conscious choices every step of the way. But we know even the smallest decisions can have a big impact when they're made with care. That's why we're always looking at how we can do better.

From printing techniques to delivery methods, we ask ourselves one simple question: would we be happy handing this world to our children?
