Six simple steps

From kit to keepsake in minutes

Our inkless kit takes the stress out of capturing those precious prints - just press, print and preserve, then leave the rest to us. Your bit is done in minutes.

01

Receive your kit

Your kit arrives with everything you need - inkless wipes, reactive paper and simple instructions. No mess, no fuss.

02

Capture those tiny prints

Gently press your little one's hand or foot onto the reactive paper. The inkless wipes are safe, and leave no residue on skin.

03

Snap a quick photo

Photograph your prints with all four corners of the paper visible. This allows us to make them true to size.

04

Upload and personalise

Use our simple upload tool to send us your prints. Choose your foil colour, card style and add any personalisation details.

05

We perfect your prints

Our team tidies any smudges whilst keeping every precious detail. We'll send you a proof to approve before we foil.

06

Treasure forever

Once approved, we foil your keepsake and post it back to you free of charge. Ready to display, ready to cherish.

the finish

That Never Fades

Our own technology, developed in our Wigan workshop, permanently bonds real metallic foil to high-quality archival card. No fading, no peeling, no speckling over time. The keepsake you open today will look just as stunning when your little one's all grown up.
