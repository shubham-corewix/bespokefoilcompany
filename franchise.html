What is the Memory Catcher™ Franchise?

A Memory Catcher is our name for a franchisee who represents Bespoke Foil Company in their local area, capturing precious prints for families in baby classes, local events, and in the comfort of their own homes.

There's no hard selling. No scripts. No pressure. The product does the talking. Once the prints are taken, we handle everything else. Your only focus is taking the prints and building relationships in your community.

Why now? As AI reshapes almost every industry, the future of business lies in real human connection. You can automate almost anything now, but you can't automate the moment someone gently uncurls a newborn's fingers to take their first print.

Babies are born every single day, and that first year happens once and never comes back. The more automated the world becomes, the more people will crave what can't be: in‑person moments, local community, and genuine relationships. That's the future we're building, and we're looking for people who see it the same way.

01

Your Commission on Every Sale (40%)

Every print you sell at a baby class, event, or home visit earns you 40% commission. On a framed print at £44.95, that's £15 profit per sale. At classes and events you'll be doing volume, so this adds up fast.

02

Affiliate Commission on Website Sales (20%)

Every Memory Catcher gets their own unique referral link and QR code. If a parent orders online later or recommends the website to a friend, any sale through your link earns you 20% commission.

03

Call-Out Charges on Home Visits (100% Yours)

For home visits or private bookings, you can add your own call-out charge on top of the product price. We suggest around £10, but it's completely up to you. Combined with your 40% commission, that's up to £25 profit per home visit.

04

Referral Bonus: Refer a New Memory Catcher (£500)

Know someone who'd be a brilliant Memory Catcher? If you refer them and they sign up, you receive £500 and they receive £500 off their setup cost.

Track your commissions in the Memory Catcher Hub

The Memory Catcher Hub is your central portal for running everything from one simple place. Track your commissions as they come in, reorder your stock, get to grips with our training videos, all in a few taps, and keep the whole business organised without the admin piling up.

It's designed to take care of the day-to-day, so you don't have to - giving you the freedom to spend more time with your family, which is what the whole Memory Catcher ethos is about.

Who we're looking for

The right person already knows what those early months feel like. The broken sleep, the appointments, the days you just need someone to talk to about all things baby. Most of our Memory Catchers have lived it themselves, and some first met us as a customer, sitting on the other side of the print.

That lived experience is the qualification. It's what lets you read the room and know when a mum needs a chat more than a sales pitch. Beyond that, we look for people who:

  • Love helping parents hold onto life's most precious moments
  • Comfortable connecting with people in your local community
  • Self-motivated with a natural drive to grow something special
  • Want a flexible business built around family life
  • Believe in the power of memories and the beauty of craftsmanship

You don't need experience and you don't need to be "salesy". The prints are beautiful, and families keep them for decades, but the print is really the by-product of something bigger: a conversation, a bit of advice, a familiar face at the class each week. Build the relationship and the rest takes care of itself.
