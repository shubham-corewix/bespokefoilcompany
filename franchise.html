What is the Memory Catcher™ Franchise?

A Memory Catcher is our name for a franchisee who represents Bespoke Foil Company in their local area, capturing precious prints for families in baby classes, local events, and in the comfort of their own homes.

There's no hard selling. No scripts. No pressure. The product does the talking. Once the prints are taken, we handle everything else. Your only focus is taking the prints and building relationships in your community.

Four Ways to Earn.One Simple Model.

Register Your Interest

01

Your Commission on Every Sale (40%)

Every print you sell at a baby class, event, or home visit earns you 40% commission. On a framed print at £44.95, that's £15 profit per sale. At classes and events you'll be doing volume, so this adds up fast.

02

Call-Out Charges on Home Visits (100% Yours)

For home visits or private bookings, you can add your own call-out charge on top of the product price. We suggest around £10, but it's completely up to you. Combined with your commission, that's up to £25 profit per home visit.

03

Affiliate Commission on Website Sales (20%)

Every Memory Catcher gets their own unique referral link and QR code. If a parent orders online later or recommends the website to a friend, any sale through your link earns you 20% commission.

04

Referral Bonus: Refer a New Memory Catcher (£500)

Know someone who'd be a brilliant Memory Catcher? If you refer them and they sign up, you receive £500 and they receive £500 off their setup cost.

Who we're looking for

This isn't your average business opportunity. We're not looking for salespeople. We're looking for warm, genuine, passionate individuals who:

  • Love helping parents hold onto life's most precious moments
  • Comfortable connecting with people in your local community
  • Self-motivated with a natural drive to grow something special
  • Want a flexible business built around family life
  • Believe in the power of memories and the beauty of craftsmanship

You don't need any experience. You don't need to be "salesy". You just need to be warm, personable, and enjoy being around families. The rest? We'll guide you every step of the way.
