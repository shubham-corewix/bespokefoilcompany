🔖 BFC Snag Tool

Drag me to your bookmarks bar - don't click me here.

  1. Browse any page of the preview site, at any viewport size.
  2. Click the bookmark - a small black panel appears top right.
  3. Press + Snag, then click the element that's wrong (it highlights as you hover). Esc cancels.
  4. Type a short note - five words is plenty.
  5. Repeat across as many pages as you like; the counter keeps score between pages.
  6. Press Export to download the snag file, and drop it into the Claude thread.

Each snag records the page, an exact CSS selector, your note, the viewport size and a text preview of the element - so there's no ambiguity about which of three identical cards you meant. Clear wipes the list once a batch has been fixed. Snags are stored in this browser on this domain only.
