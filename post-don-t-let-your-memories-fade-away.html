Don't Let Your Memories Fade Away

Ashley Eccleston 22 September 2025 · 3 min read

When our little ones arrive, life becomes a swirl of soft blankets, tiny yawns, and unforgettable firsts.

  • The first time their fingers curl around yours.
  • That sleepy smile in the middle of a feed.
  • The indescribable moment when everything shifts and life begins to revolve around someone new.

These moments matter, and it's no wonder we want to capture them before they quietly slip away.

That's why we started The Bespoke Foil Company - to help families hold onto those fleeting memories in a way that truly lasts.

But there's something many parents don't realise until it's too late - not all keepsake prints are built to stand the test of time.

A Fading Memory - Ryan's unexpected discovery

A few months ago, Ryan, one of our co-founders and a proud dad himself, noticed something deeply upsetting in our studio. During one of Ashley's local baby classes, a lovely set of hand and footprint impressions had been created using standard inkless wipes on reactive paper - the kind that's often included in cheaper hand & footprint keepsake kits.

The prints were originally set aside on a shelf, but when Ryan came across them just a few weeks later, he noticed they had already started to fade.

Curious (and more than a little concerned), he began a quiet little experiment - photographing the same print each month to see what would happen over time.

The results were heart-breaking.

Within just 12 months, that once-dark, crisp print of a tiny hand had lost its shape. The gentle lines blurred. The unique little toes disappeared into the background. What once felt like a moment frozen in time had faded into something barely visible.

Most Parents Won't Realise Until It's Too Late

That was the most painful part.

If this had been your little one's only set of hand or footprint memories, the moment would now be gone. And that's the danger of simple inkless print kits that don't apply any lasting finish.

They might look beautiful at first, but they fade. Sometimes in a matter of weeks. And by the time you realise, your little one is crawling, walking, talking - and the chance to capture that newborn magic has passed.

We Made a Promise - Your memories will never fade

This experience only strengthened our belief in doing things differently. At The Bespoke Foil Company, our kit includes inkless wipes and reactive paper - but that's only the beginning.

Once you've captured your baby's prints, you upload them to our simple, user-friendly Upload Portal. There, you choose your foil colour, wording, layout and frame style. And then, something special happens...

We take your little one's prints and edit them into digital, foil-ready artwork, which means two things:

  1. We will always have a digital version of your prints that we can refoil, should the worst happen and you misplace your original. We archive all prints - never delete, they remain backed up on our system forever, we still have the first foil hand and footprint we ever produced on file
  2. We can foil additional replica copies with your foil-ready artwork, perfect gifts for family members such as Grandma and Grandad

Once you approve your digital artwork, we then use our exclusive Foil Fusion process - a technique we've spent over five years perfecting.

Unlike toner foiling, which sits on top of the card and breaks down over time, Foil Fusion fuses directly into the fibres of the card. No glue. No shortcuts. Just a smooth, satin foil finish that will never fade, peel or crack.

It's built to last. For your home. For their childhood. For a lifetime.

This is Not Just a Kit - It's a memory that shines forever

Yes, our Foil Hand & Footprint Kit might be a little more than the budget options you'll find online. But what we offer isn't just a product - it's peace of mind. It's knowing your baby's first memories are preserved with care, emotion and quality that never fades.

You won't be left years later with a faded blur of what once was. Instead, you'll have something to smile at every time you pass it on the wall. Something your child will one day treasure too.

When You Choose Us - You join our story

We're not a faceless brand. We're a small family business, made up of real parents who understand how precious those first moments are. Every product we make is handled with the kind of care we'd want for our own families.

Because when time moves so quickly, it matters that your memories don't fade.

Ashley & Ryan
