## 3. What changed in this build

### Your items from the testing document

**Form submissions (items 1 and 2).** `submit-lead.js` rewritten around a FORMS
registry. Each form now owns its subject line, email template, CAPI config and
target table.

The email was worse than "confusing": **`notes` was rendered nowhere.** The
contact form puts the customer's whole message in `notes`, the slot form puts
the baby's name there, the MC enquiry puts the entire enquiry detail there.
Three forms were writing to a field nothing read. The email now renders **every**
field submitted, so this cannot recur for a new form. Validation is per form, so
no form needs placeholder values to pass.

CAPI: you were right on both counts. It fired for everything, and `3445` is the
franchise Founders Pricing rather than a test leftover. Ryan's decision - all
forms except the community sign-up count as a Lead. Franchise and mc-enquiry
carry 3445; contact and slot fire with **no `value` key at all**, not zero. A
zero still enters value-based bidding as a datapoint worth nothing.

Storage: `leads` plus `community_signups`. Community is its own table as you
asked, `email` unique with `merge-duplicates`, so a repeat sign-up updates the
row rather than firing your welcome automation twice. RLS on both, no anon
policy - service role only. Written before the email, so a Mandrill outage costs
a notification rather than a lead.

**Our Story video (item 4). Fixed.** The click listener was on the frame and the
video mounts inside that frame, so every click on the native pause control
bubbled up and built a fresh autoplaying video. It was not restarting - it was a
different element each time. `arm()` / `disarm()` now takes the listener off the
moment the video exists. **Reuse this pattern for the Hub training videos rather
than writing a new one.**

**Franchisee login (item 3).** Scoped, not built. See `MC-HUB-V1-BRIEF.md`.

## 4. New build guards - read before your next change

Six added today. Each was written after a real bug reached a preview or the live
site, and each was verified by deliberately breaking it and watching the build
fail. **If one of these stops your build, it has probably found something real.**

| Guard | Fails when |
|---|---|
| `check-footer` | a `<header>` or `<footer>` carrying a class does not declare its own `position` / `background` - the bare element selectors in `shared/styles.css` otherwise apply and drop it on top of the content, or give it a black background |
| `check-forms` | anything posting to `submit-lead` sends no valid `formType`; or a `data-inc="x"` trigger has no `<dialog id="inc-x">`; or `<dialog>` tags do not balance |
| `check-internal-links` | an `<a href="#">` with visible text goes nowhere. Anchors whose id is assigned a href in the same file are excused, so the region map's `cardMore` still passes |
| `check-analytics` | a hand-drawn Apple Pay button exists anywhere |
| `build-trustpilot` | `.tp-sep` uses a fixed colour instead of `currentColor` - it disappears against one of the two hero backgrounds |

Comments are stripped before matching in all of them, so a comment explaining a
rule cannot satisfy it.
