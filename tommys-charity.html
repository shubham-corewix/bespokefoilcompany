Giving Back

Every keepsake helps bring more babies home

For every kit we sell, we donate £1 to Tommy's, the leading charity funding pregnancy research in the UK. A small gesture on its own, but together, it adds up to something that matters.

£1

From every single kit, to a cause close to our hearts

We started this business to help families hold onto the moments that pass far too quickly. So it felt only right that our work should also help the families who don't get to make those memories.

That's why £1 from every kit we sell goes straight to Tommy's. You don't have to do anything extra, it's simply part of every order, our way of turning your keepsake into a little bit of good.

Who are Tommy's?

Tommy's is the pregnancy and baby charity, and the leading charity funding pregnancy research in the UK. Their mission is to stop the heartbreak and devastation of baby loss, and to make pregnancy and birth safe for everyone.

They fund ground-breaking research into the causes of miscarriage, stillbirth and premature birth, and provide expert, midwife-led information and support for families at every stage of their journey.

You can learn more about their work at tommys.org.

Why it matters to us

As parents ourselves, we know how precious those early days are, and we know that not every family gets to keep them. That truth sits with us in everything we make.

When you order a kit, you're not just preserving your own memory. You're helping fund the research and the care that gives other families the chance to make theirs. It's the part of what we do that we're most proud of.

Thank you for being part of it.

Ashley & Ryan

Co-founders, The Bespoke Foil Company
