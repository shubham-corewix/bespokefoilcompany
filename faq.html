About Our Handprint & Footprint Kits

Our foil hand and footprint kits are mess-free, stress-free ways to capture your baby's tiny hand or footprints at home. The kit includes everything you need to take clear prints using safe, inkless wipes and special reactive paper. We then transform these prints into stunning foil keepsakes using our exclusive Foil Fusion Technology.

Each kit contains:

  • 2 inkless, baby-safe wipes
  • 4 sheets of A5 reactive paper
  • Detailed instruction booklet
  • Your order number card (needed for uploading photos)
  • One A4 framed foil print included (delivered free)

Each kit allows you to create prints in one of these combinations:

  • Two footprints
  • One handprint and one footprint
  • Two handprints (usually siblings)

Taking the prints takes less than 5 minutes! It's designed for busy parents and can even be done while your baby is sleeping.

Our Foil Fusion Technology

We're the only company using Foil Fusion Technology - our exclusive process that seamlessly bonds foil to premium cardstock. Unlike traditional methods that glue foil on top (causing speckles and peeling), our process creates a flawless, durable finish that lasts a lifetime.

No! Our Foil Fusion Technology permanently bonds the foil to the card, ensuring no peeling, fading, or deterioration. Your keepsake is designed to last generations.

Delivery & Process

  1. Take photos of your prints
  2. Upload them to our website with your order number
  3. Choose your personalisation options
  4. The editing process at our end begins - cleaning up any smudges
  5. We send you proofs for approval via email or WhatsApp
  6. Once approved, we create your foil keepsake
  7. We deliver it to your door free of charge

After you approve your digital proof, we aim to dispatch within 5 working days. During busy periods it may take slightly longer, but we'll keep you updated.

Currently, we ship only to the UK.

Comparisons & Alternatives

DIY methods using ink or paint are messy, can irritate baby's skin, and often produce smudged results. Our kits are mess-free, completely safe, and produce professional-quality results every time.

Clay and plaster require babies to stay still longer, can be messy, and the results are fragile and easily broken. Our foil prints are durable, can be reproduced if needed, and the process is much gentler for babies.
